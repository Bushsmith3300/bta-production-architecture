How the Final Semester Period Works

When a semester ends before the student's normal monthly billing cycle is completed, the billing period ends on the actual semester end date.

For example, if a student's billing date is 18 December and the semester ends on 31 December, the displayed billing period is:

18 December – 31 December

The remaining days are used to determine the applicable semester proration according to the school's proration rules.

Semester Proration Rules
  • 1–7 days remaining: 25% of monthly fee
  • 8–14 days remaining: 50% of monthly fee
  • 15–21 days remaining: 75% of monthly fee
  • 22+ days remaining: 100% of monthly fee
